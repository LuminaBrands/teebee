TeeBee

A calm, parent-controlled home screen for Google TV, made for families with toddlers. This is an early prototype. Thank you for trying it!

Download TeeBee

For Google TV devices. On your TV, use the Downloader app (steps below).

Install on your Google TV

  1. On the TV, open the Google Play Store, search for Downloader and install it.
  2. Open Downloader. When it asks, allow it to install apps. If it doesn't ask: Settings › Apps › Security & restrictions › Unknown sources, turn on Downloader.
  3. In Downloader's address box, type luminabrands.github.io/teebee and select Go.
  4. Select Download TeeBee, then Install.
  5. Open TeeBee. Its setup helps you turn on Display over other apps (the timer over other apps) and Notification access (to know when an episode ends).

Then

  1. Scan the code on the TV with your phone's camera (phone and TV on the same Wi-Fi) and pick shows and movies.
  2. Keep Home on the remote on TeeBee: press ↑ ↑ ↓ ↓ › Keep the Home button on TeeBee, then switch on TeeBee Home button.

Grown-ups: press Up, Up, Down, Down on the remote for TeeBee's settings.
Updating: download and install again; your shows and movies stay. When TeeBee arrives in Google Play, uninstall this version first.
